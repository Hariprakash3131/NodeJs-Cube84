Legal & Security Governance

Privacy Notice & Data Protection Policy

Last revised: November 4, 2026 • Version 2.4

1. Scope and Commitments

Servora Inc. ("Servora", "we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting confidential organizational telemetry, personal data, and technical specifications provided through our consulting engagements and digital platforms.

2. Information Collection

We collect only information necessary to deliver contracted architectural and engineering services:

  • Identity & Corporate Data: Full name, professional corporate email, organization name, role title.
  • Project Briefs: Technical scopes, architecture requirements, and milestone objectives.
  • Client Telemetry: Log event metrics and performance telemetry explicitly provisioned by client agents.

3. GDPR & International Compliance

In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users possess the right to access, rectify, export, or permanently delete personal data stored across our systems. Requests may be directed to privacy@servora.io.

4. Cryptographic Security Standards

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3 with forward secrecy. Data at rest is encrypted via AES-256-GCM. We maintain continuous automated penetration testing and annual SOC 2 Type II audit attestations.
