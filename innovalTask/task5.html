Welcome to Ooty

Ooty is a beautiful hill station in Tamil Nadu, famous for its pleasant weather, tea gardens, lakes, and scenic mountains.

Popular Tourist Places

Ooty Lake is a popular boating destination.

Doddabetta Peak is the highest mountain in the Nilgiris.

The Botanical Garden is home to many rare plants and flowers.
