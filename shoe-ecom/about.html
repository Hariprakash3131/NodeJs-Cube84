Our Evolution

FROM BESPOKE WORKSHOP TO GLOBAL ICON

Take a look behind the curtain at the major milestones that defined the KickVault heritage.

01
2020 • Inception

The Kinetic Blueprint

Founded in a Brooklyn loft by two marathon runners and industrial designers dissatisfied with mass-produced synthetic midsoles. The prototype sole earned its first marathon podium in Boston.

02
2023 • Expansion

Global Vault Launch

KickVault.com officially opened its digital doors with real-time deadstock verification. The first drop of 10,000 limited pairs sold out in precisely 6 minutes across North America, London, and Tokyo.

03
2026 • Present

Circular Future

Achieving 100% carbon-neutral fulfillment with circular footwear recycling programs and carbon-fiber propulsion plates tested by over 500,000 active athletes and tastemakers.
